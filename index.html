SUNSET RUSH · 落日狂飙

A lightweight WebGL racing demo. Click Play to open the playable page (works best on desktop browsers with WebGL enabled).

Play (Open in new tab)

Controls: ← → or A D to steer, Enter to start, P or Esc to pause. Touch: use on-screen buttons if shown.

If the demo fails to load, ensure your browser supports WebGL and allows loading external scripts from CDNs (three.js).
